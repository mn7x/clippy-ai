Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

These rules are built into Clippy for every user and cannot be switched off. They apply to moment picking, titles, descriptions, captions, dubbing, Trending, search and everything the App makes. By using Clippy you agree to follow them.

1. Islam is a red line

Any content that insults, curses, mocks, attacks or speaks badly of Allah, the Holy Quran, the Prophets and Messengers, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his Sunnah, the Companions, the Tabi'in, the trusted scholars of Ahl al-Sunnah, or Islamic practices such as prayer, fasting and hijab is prohibited, whether serious, satirical, or framed as a "debate" or "meme".

2. Palestine

Any support, praise, justification, normalisation or positive presentation of Israel, its occupation, government, leaders, army or settlers, or campaigns in its support is prohibited; anything about Palestine is presented from the perspective of the Palestinian people's rights.

3. Prohibited content

Sexual content of any kind, including nudity, innuendo and sexual jokes; graphic violence and glorifying violence, terrorism or harm; stirring sectarian, tribal, regional or ethnic strife between Muslims or Muslim countries, and takfir; promoting gambling, betting, alcohol, drugs, smoking, magic, sorcery or astrology; profanity, insults, bullying and harassment; any abuse or exploitation of children; violating others' privacy or publishing their data; fraud, deception, fake news and impersonating people or organisations; and any illegal activity.

4. Misusing the App

You may not: try to disable or bypass the rules, messages or updates; tamper with the App or its servers or flood them with requests; use it to harvest others' data or for spam; or use it in breach of platform terms, such as re-uploading others' content without the right or faking views.

5. What happens on a violation

The App may refuse the request, blur parts of a clip, leave out moments or whole clips, or hide topics from Trending and search, automatically and without notice. For repeated or serious violations we may end your licence and block the services linked to your installation.
