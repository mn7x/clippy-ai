AI Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Clippy relies on AI models from outside providers. They are powerful but not infallible, and we want you to know their limits before relying on their results.

1. How it works

Models are used to transcribe speech, pick moments, write titles, descriptions and tags, translate and dub, locate faces and the streamer's camera, and screen content against the rules. Results are automatic and probabilistic; they can contain mistakes in words, timing, translation or content judgement, and can differ between runs and between models.

2. Review before publishing

Review every clip, text and translation before publishing it; you are responsible for what you publish. Dubbing or translating other people's voices or content may require their permission; never use any feature to mislead, impersonate, or create fake content attributed to real people.

3. Data sent

Only the needed parts (text, audio or frames) are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose, with your key, under that provider's retention and use policy. Clippy does not send your content to any model you did not choose.
