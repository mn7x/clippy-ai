Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

Privacy is part of Clippy's design: your videos stay on your device, we do not sell your data, and we collect the minimum needed to count users, deliver messages and updates, and answer people who write to us. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it and what your rights are.

1. What stays on your device only

Videos, streams, clips, projects, captions and work files; AI provider keys and platform keys (stored encrypted); sign-in data from the built-in browser such as your YouTube session; your settings, styles and brand kits. None of this is uploaded to Clippy's servers. If you link a server you own, this data moves to that server and is managed by you.

Our commitment on keys and accounts: we never take, collect, store, sell, leak or look at your API keys, passwords, tokens or account sessions. They are encrypted with AES-256 on your device and are sent only directly to the service they belong to (for example Groq, Google Gemini or YouTube) when you use it. Clippy's team has no technical way to read them, and we will never ask you to send them to us — anyone who does is not us.

2. What reaches Clippy's server

When the App runs: a random id for this installation that is not linked to your name or any account, the app version, interface language, operating system name, the country derived from the connection (we do not store the IP address itself), run times in aggregate, and which team messages you have read.

When you write to us from "Contact us": your message, the category you choose, your name and contact if you type them yourself, and technical details (app version, system, recent errors) if you leave that option on. We do not collect or ask for sensitive data, so do not put it in your messages.

Crash reports: when an error or crash happens, a technical report may be sent to our error-tracking system (GlitchTip) containing only the error message and where it happened in the code (with the user's folder name removed), the app version, the job type, the operating system and the random installation id — never any video, text, key or personal data — so we can fix faults quickly.

3. Why we use the data

To count users and see versions, countries and languages in aggregate; to deliver the right messages and updates to each version, country and language; to force an update when there is a security or technical fault; to answer support messages, fix bugs and improve the App; and to protect the App and its servers from abuse. We do not use it for targeted advertising or to build personal profiles.

4. Third-party services

AI features send the needed text, audio or video frames directly from your device to the provider you chose (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq or others) with your key, under that provider's privacy policy. Downloading videos and streams and showing Trending connects directly to the platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Kick and others) under their policies. Clippy's server runs on Cloudflare and the download page and releases are on GitHub. We recommend reading those parties' policies.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. We disclose it only to the hosting providers needed to run the service and only as far as necessary, when the law or a valid court order requires it, to protect our rights and users' safety, or as part of an orderly business transfer with the same level of protection.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

We keep aggregate usage data as long as needed for statistics and support messages as long as needed to help you, then delete or anonymise them. You may ask to see, correct or delete the data linked to your installation, or object to its processing, by emailing support@mn7.online with details that help us identify your installation. Removing the App removes your local data from your device.

7. Children

The App is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data about them. If you learn a child has sent us data, contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, encryption for stored keys, and protect the control panel with a password, extra verification and login logging. No system is one hundred percent secure; if an incident affects users' data, we will tell those affected without undue delay.
