Last updated: 2026-11-21

Clippy is a clip-making tool; we respect content creators' rights and expect you to do the same. This policy explains your responsibility, our role and how to report.

1. Your responsibility

Clipping and publishing other people's content may require their permission. Use only content you own, hold a licence or explicit permission to use (such as some creators' official clipping programmes), or the law allows you to use, such as fair quotation, criticism and commentary. Always credit the original creator, add real value through commentary, editing or explanation, and never re-upload as is. Follow the terms and copyright systems of the platforms you download from or publish to.

2. Clippy's role

Clippy is software that runs on your device or your server; it does not host clips or publish them for you. We grant no rights in anyone else's content, do not verify your rights before use, and are not responsible for any infringement you commit.

3. Clippy's property

The Clippy name, logo, design, interfaces, code, caption styles, templates and documentation belong to MN7 Company and are protected. Do not use them in a way that suggests you represent us or that we endorse you without written permission. Bundled open-source fonts, icons and libraries remain under their own licences.

4. Reporting an infringement

If you believe the App itself or material we published infringes your rights, email support@mn7.online with: your identity and contact, a description of the protected work, where the infringing material is, and a statement of good faith and accuracy. We will review the report and act appropriately. Clips users publish on platforms should be reported to those platforms directly.
